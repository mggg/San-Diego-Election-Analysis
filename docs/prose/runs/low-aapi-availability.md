Although the AAPI share of the voting age population is relatively high in San Diego, the candidate pools in the last several San Diego city elections do not feature AAPI candidates at a rate proportional to the AAPI electorate. In our Basic 3 X 3 scenario, we assume the candidate availability in each slate is roughly proportional to bloc VAP share. This scenario runs a 3 X 3 STV election to simulate a lower-than-proportional rate of AAPI candidate availability within each district. For comparison, the average per-district AAPI candidate availability within the Basic scenario is 0.98 while here it is 0.32.
