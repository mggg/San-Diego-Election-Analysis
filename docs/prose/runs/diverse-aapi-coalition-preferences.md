San Diego has a diverse and varied Asian population, with significant shares of the AAPI coalition belonging to ethnically Filipino, Vietnamese, and Chinese voters - amongst many others. As such, we believe its reasonable to assume that their electoral and candidate preferences vary as well. In this scenario, we simulate this by adjusting the Diriclet alpha value for the AAPI bloc to reflect a more uniform preference distribution amongst available AAPI-preferred candidates. In other words, if we assume that the candidates in the AAPI slate reflect the diversity of the AAPI bloc, we'd expect support to be more evenly divided among them.
