Within California in 2024, Asian voters were observed to have voted at rate of 44.4%, while White and Black voters voted at rates of 56.1% and 60.1%, respectively. Low voter turnout among the AAPI coalition has been a consistent concern for civic and community activists in San Diego. In this scenario, we adjust the turnout parameter for the AAPI bloc to be even lower than the default configuration, from 0.75 to 0.5. This means that the relative turnout rate of AAPI voters is half that of White and Black voters. We examine the impacts of low AAPI turnout within a 3 X 3 STV electoral system on proportional representation.
