Often times, elections using a form of RCV limit the number of candidates that voters can rank on their ballots - even for elections with large candidate pools. In this scenario, we ask how the electoral system modeled in the City Charter Amendment Proposal would be impacted if we set a minimum and maximum ballot length - specifically in the 1 X 6 STV component of the election. We run two different simulations; one of them sets the maximum ballot length to six, and the other pins it to 10 ranked candidates. In each simulation, we enforce a minimum ballot length of six since we have six at-large seats available.
