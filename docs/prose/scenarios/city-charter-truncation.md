Elections that use ranked ballots often limit how many candidates a voter may rank, even when the candidate pool is large. In this scenario, we ask what such a limit would mean for the electoral system in the City Charter Amendment Proposal. We generate one set of ballots in which every voter ranks every candidate, shown here as Full, and then cut each ballot in the 1 X 6 STV component to its first N choices, for N from 10 down to 4. Because every option uses the same ballots, any difference between options comes from the ballot-length limit alone. The nine single-winner IRV districts are left untouched, so their results, and the candidate pools in every district, are the same under every option. Use the Ballot length toggle to move from Full to 4.
